StorageFinished product: Store in a refrigerator (2°C - 8°C). Do not freeze the reconstituted solution. Aseptic reconstitution and dilution A vial of herzuma reconstituted with bacteriostatic water for injection containing 1.1% benzyl alcohol as a preservative (supplied) is stable for 28 days at 2- 8°C after reconstitution and must not be frozen. The appropriate amount of solution should be withdrawn from the vial using a sterile needle and syringe added to a polyvinylchloride, polyethylene or polypropylene infusion bag containing 250ml of 0.9% sodium chloride solution. Once the infusion is prepared, it should be administered immediately. If diluted aspetically, it may be stored for 24 hours (do not store above 30°C Swirl vial gently to aid reconstitution. DO NOT SHAKE If herzuma is reconstituted with SWFI without preservative, use immediately and discard any unused portion.
