StorageFinished Drug Product: Store at temperature not exceeding 30°C, keep in the original pack to protect from light. Active Drug substance: Preserve in airtight container, protected from light at temperature not exceeding 25°C. After Reconstitution: For IM injection: It should be reconstituted with 1.5 ml WFI and to be used immediately or to be stored for 24 hours at room temperature, not exceeding 25°C or to be stored for 7 days at temperature (2-8) °C. For Slow IV injection: It should be reconstituted with 5 ml WFI or any of other compatible IV diluents (0.9% NaCl solution- 0.9% NaCl + 5% glucose solution- Ringer solution- Ringer solution + 5% glucose solution- 5% glucose solution- 10% glucose solution- sodium lactate 6M solution) and to be used immediately or to be stored for 24 hours at room temperature, not exceeding 25°C or to be stored for 7 days at temperature (2-8) °C. For IV infusion: It should be diluted after reconstitution with one of the compatible IV diluents (WFI - 0.9% NaCl solution- 0.9% NaCl + 5% glucose solution- Ringer solution- Ringer solution + 5% glucose solution- 5% glucose solution- 10% glucose solution- sodium lactate 6M solution) and to be used immediately and to be infused over a period of 30 minutes.
