StorageBefore reconstitution: Stored at temperature not exceeding 30 °C. After reconstitution: to 5ml using water for injection, vials can be stored up to 24 hours at temperature not exceeding 30 °C. After dilution with one of the infusion solution: store diluted azithromycin solution for infusion for 24 hours at temperature not exceeding 30°C or at refrigerated temperature (2 °C -8 °C) for 72 hours. Dilution solutions for infusion: "250 or 500 ml of 0.9% sodium chloride, 0.45% sodium chloride, 5% dextrose in water, lactated Ringer's solution, 5% dextrose in 0.3% sodium chloride , 5% dextrose in 0.45 % sodium chloride and administrated as IV infusion over at least 60 minutes".
