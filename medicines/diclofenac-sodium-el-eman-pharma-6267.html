StorageStore at temperature not exceeding 30 °C.Protect from light. For I.M Injection: used immediately after opening. For I.V Infusion: Used immediately after dilution with 0.9 % sodium chloride or 5 % glucose and the diluted solution must have buffered with sodium bicarbonate solution (0.5 ml 8.4 % or 1 ml 4.2 %). For treatment of moderate to severe post operative pain: It should be infused continuously over a period of 30 min. to 2 hrs. For the preventon of post operative pain: a loading dose of 25 mg-50 mg should be infused after surgery over 15 min to 1 hr.
