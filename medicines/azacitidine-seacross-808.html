StorageStore in dry place in a temperature not exceeding 25°C. When azaciditine seacross is reconditioned using water for injections that has not been refrigerated, chemical & physical in-use stability of the reconstituted medicinal product has been demonstrated at 25 °C for 45 minutes and at 2 °C to 8 °C for 8 hours. The shelf life of the reconditioned medicinal product can be extended by reconstituting with refrigerated (2? to 8?) water for injection. When azaciditine seacross is reconstituted using water for injections that has not been refrigerated, chemical & physical in-use stability of the reconstituted medicinal product has been demonstrated at 2 °Cto 8 °C for 22 hours. From microbiological point of view, the reconstituted product should be used immediately, if not used immediately, in-use storage times & condition prior to use are the responsibility of the user & must be no longer than 8 hours at 2 °C to 8 °C when reconstituted using water for injection that has not been refrigerated or not longer than 22 hours when reconstituted using refrigerated (2 °C to 8 °C) water for injection.
