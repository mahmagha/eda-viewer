StorageDon't Store above 25°C, Protect from light. Precautions: The stability of the following methotrexate solutions has been tested under light protection, at 25 °C (room temperature) over 24 hours: 5% glucose solution 0.9% Sodium chloride solution The Chemical and physical stability of the ready for use preparation was demonstrated for 28 days at 2 °C to 8 °C and at room temperature(20-25 °C ) with protection from light From microbiological viewpoint, the ready to use preparation to be used immediately. If the ready to used preparation is not used immediately, the user is responsible for the duration and condition of storage. If the ready to use solution is not prepared under controlled and validated aseptic conditions, it must not be stored for longer than 24 hours at 2 °C to 8 °C -For single use only discard any surplus -The solution must be visually inspected first before use -Only clear solutions, which practically free of particles should be used Known incompatibilities: -Strong oxidants, strong acid, chlorpromazine HCl, droperidol, idarubicin, metoclopramide HCl, Prednisolone sodium phosphate, Promethazine HCl -Incompatibilities of methotrexate with Cytarabine and flurouracil have been reported, however the incompatibility with fluorouracil is in doubt -Incompatibilities with other substances may occur if the solution is stored for more than 24 hours
