StorageDo not store above 25 °C. Store the ampoules in the outer carton in order to protect from light. After first opening the medicinal product should be used immediately. From microbiological point of view, the diluted solution should be used immediately, if not used immediately in use storage and conditions are the responsibility of the user and would be normally no longer than 24 hours at 2-8°C, unless dilution has taken place in controlled and validated aseptic conditions.
