StorageStore in a refrigerator (2°C-8°C), Do not freeze. The color of the concentrate may turn to orange during storage, but there is no loss of potency and the product may be used until the indicated expiry date if storage instructions have been followed. For single use only. The medicinal product should be visually inspected prior to use. Only clear solution free from particles should be used. Approved In-use Shelf -life & Storage Condition: Once opened, the medicinal product should be used immediately, any remaining contents should be discarded. Pharmaceutical product should be diluted with the following: To prepare the 0.025 mg/ml infusion ,mix 5 ml of Levosimendan – Sunny Pharmaceutical with 500 ml of 5% glucose solution .To prepare the 0.05 mg/ml infusion , mix 10 ml of Levosimendan – Sunny Pharmaceutical with 500 ml of 5% glucose solution. The diluted solution should be used within 24 hours at 2-8°C or within 24 hours at 25°C and inspect the diluted solution visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
