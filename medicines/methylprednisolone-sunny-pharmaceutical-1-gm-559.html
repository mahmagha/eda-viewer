StorageStore at temperature not exceeding 30°C After reconstitution with 15.6 ml of water for injection, the solution should be clear and colorless. Used immediately and discard any remainder. For IV infusion the initially prepared solution may be diluted with 100 ml 5% Dextrose in water, 100 ml Isotonic Saline solution, or 100 ml 5% Dextrose in Isotonic Saline solution The diluted solution should be used within 48 hours when stored at 25°C.
