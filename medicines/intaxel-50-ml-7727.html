StorageDon't store above 25°c, Protect from light. Precaution: After opening before dilution: Chemical & Physical in use stability has been demonstrated for 28 days at 25°C following multiple needle entries & product withdrawal, other in use storage times & conditions are the responsibility of the user. After dilution: chemical & Physical in use, stability of the solution prepared for infusion has been demonstrated at 25°C for 24 hours when diluted in 5% glucose solution, 0.9% Sodium chloride, 5% glucose solution in Ringer solution ,5% glucose solution /0.9% sodium chloride solution. From Microbiological point of view, the diluted product should be used immediately, if not used immediately, in use storage times & conditions prior to use arc the responsibility of the user & would normally not be longer than 24 hours at 2 °C to 8 °C unless dilution has taken place in controlled & validated aseptic conditions. After dilution the solution is for single use only.
