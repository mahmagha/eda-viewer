StorageDon’t store above 25°C, don’t refrigerate or freeze. The only approved solvent for preparation of sterile gemcitabine powder is sodium chloride 9 mg/ ml (0.9%) solution for injection (without preservative). For reconstitution, add 5 ml sodium chloride 9 mg/ml (0.9%). Chemical and physical stability was demonstrated for 24 hours at 25°C. For Microbiological reasons, the solution should be used immediately, the user is responsible for retention time and conditions. This should normally not be more than 24 hours at room temperature, unless the solution and further dilution (if applicable) were carried out under validated aseptic conditions. The prepared Gemcitabine solution must not be kept refrigerated as precipitation may occur.
