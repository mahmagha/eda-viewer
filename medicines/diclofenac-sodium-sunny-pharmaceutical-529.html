StorageStore at temperature not exceeding 30°C. Keep the ampoule in the carton box in order to protect from light. The infusion solution shouldn’t be used if crystals or precipitates are observed. In case of intravenous infusion: immediately before initiating an intravenous infusion, the solution must be diluted with 100-500 ml of either sodium chloride 0.9% or glucose 5% both solutions should be buffered with sodium bicarbonate solution (0.5ml 8.4% or 1 ml 4.2%). Only clear solutions should be used for 24 hours when stored at temperature not exceeding 25°C. In case of injection: the product should be used immediately.
