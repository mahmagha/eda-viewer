StorageFinished Product: - Store at temperature (2-8oC). - Store in the original carton to protect from light. - Do not freeze. After Reconstitution: - Besponsa do not contain bacteriostatic preservatives. Use reconstituted solution immediately or after being refrigerated (2-8oC) for up to 4 hours. Protect from light and do not freeze. - Gently swirl the vial to aid dissolution, don't shake. Diluted solution- After start of dilution: - The Diluted solution must be used immediately or after stored at room temperature (20-25oC) for up to 4 hours, or in a refrigerator (2–8°C) for up to 3 hours. The maximum time from reconstitution through the end of administration should be ? 8 hours, with ? 4 hours between reconstitution and dilution. Protect from light and do not freeze. Diluted solution (Administration): - If the diluted solution is refrigerated (2–8°C; 36–46°F), allow it to equilibrate at room temperature (20–25°C; 68–77°F) for approximately 1 hour prior to administration. Administer diluted solution within 8 hours of reconstitution as a 1 hour infusion at a rate of 50 mL/h at room temperature (20–25°C; 68–77°F). Protect from light.
