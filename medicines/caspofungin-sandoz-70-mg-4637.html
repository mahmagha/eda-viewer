StorageStore unopened vials in the refrigerator (2°C - 8°C). Concentrate solution : Prepared by adding 10.5 ml of water for injection to the vial to obtain solution of concentration of 7.2mg/ml , should be used immediately , physical and chemical stability data have shown that the concentrate solution can be stored for up to 24 houres when stored at temperature not exceeding 25°C Diluted infusion solution : Prepared by adding appropriate amount of concentrate solution to 250ml of sodium chloride solution 9mg/ml (0.9%) or lactated ringer solution , should be used immediately , physical and chemical stability data have shown that the product can be stored for up to 24 houres when stored at temperature not exceeding 25°C or within 48 houres when intravenous infusion bag (bottle) stored refrigerated at (2°C - 8°C). The product contains no preservatives . from a microbiological perspective , the product should be used immediately . if not used immediately , the storage times & conditions for use are the responsibility of the user and would normally not be longer than 24 houres at (2°C - 8°C). , unless reconstitution and dilution have taken place in controlled validated aseptic conditions.
