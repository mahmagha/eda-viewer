StorageDon’t store above 30 °C, Do not freeze and keep the vial in outer carton to protect from light. After Opening prior to dilution:, the concentrate should be used immediately After Dilution in 5% glucose: The chemical and physical in-use stability of the diluted solution is demonstrated for 24 hours if stored at 2 – 8 °C and stable for 6 hours if stored at 25°C. From a microbiological point of view, the diluted solution should be used immediately. If not used immediately, in-use storage and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user and would normally not be longer than 24 hours at 2 °C to 8 °C unless dilution has taken place in controlled and validated aseptic conditions.
