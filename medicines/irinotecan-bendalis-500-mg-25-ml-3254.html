StorageUnopened Vial: Store below 25°C, don’t freeze, Store in the original package to protect from light. Precautions: Irinotecan Bendalis Concentrate for solution for infusion intended for intravenous infusion only after dilution with recommended solvents: either 0.9% Sodium chloride solution for infusion or 5% glucose solution for infusion. If any precipitate is visible in the vial or after reconstitution, the medicinal product should be discarded in accordance with standard procedures for cytostatic agents. From a microbiological point of view, the product should be used immediately after dilution, even if the method of opening and dilution rule out the possibility of microbiological contamination. If not used immediately, in-use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user.
