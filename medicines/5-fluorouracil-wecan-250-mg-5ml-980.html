StorageDon`t store above over 25°C, don`t store in fridge, protect contents against light & only for one time removal Shelf life after dilution: Dilution is possible using 0.9% sodium chloride solution or 5% glucose solution. Stability data at concentrations of (0.35 mg/ml and 15 mg/ml) have shown that the maximum storage period for prepared 5-Fluorouracil infusion solutions is 28 days.This storage period refers both to cooling in refrigerator (2°C to 8°C) including light protection and storage at room temperature (20°C to 25°C) with or without light protection. From microbiological perspective, the prepared solution can be used immediately. If not used directly, the storage conditions of the prepared infusion solution are the responsibility of the user prior to usage and will normally not exceed 24 hours at (2°C to 8°C) unless the dilution has taken place under controlled and validated aseptic conditions.
