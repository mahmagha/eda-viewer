In- use Precautions: Preparation of the solution from Holoxan: For preparation of the 4 % strength, ready- to- inject solution, the following amount of water for injection is added to the powder, depending on the pack size: Powder: 1 gm Water for injections: 25 ml For intravenous infusion (approx. 30-120 min) the solution is diluted in 250 ml of ringer solution or 5 % strength glucose solution or 0.9% strength physiological saline solution. For prolonged administration, over one to two hours, dilution with 500 ml ringer solution or 5% strength glucose solution or 0.9% strength physiological saline solution. For continuous 24 hours infusion with high doses of ifosfamide, the solution, e.g. 5g/m2, is diluted in 3 liters of 5% glucose solution or 0.9% physiological saline. *Chemical and physical in-use stability has been demonstrated for 48 hours at 25?. From a microbiological point of view, the ready -for -use preparation should be used immediately, the in-use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user. Storage would normally not be longer than 24 hours at 2 to 8? unless reconstitution has taken place under controlled and validated aseptic conditions.
