StorageFor Active Drug Substance: preserved in tight containers and store in a cool and dry place and protected from light at temperature not exceeding 20°C For Finished Drug Product: Stored at temperature not exceeding 25°C After reconstitution: The reconstituted solutions for intravenous injection or infusion should be used immediately. The time interval between the beginning of reconstitution and the end of intravenous injection or infusion should not exceed one hour. For Injection: Raktonem 1000 mg to be used for bolus intravenous injection should be constituted with 20 ml sterile water for injection. For infusion: For intravenous infusion meropenem vials may be directly constituted with sodium chloride (0.9 %) solution for infusion or glucose (5 %) solution for infusion, to a final concentration of I to 20 mg/ml. Each vial is for single use only. Standard aseptic techniques should be used for solution preparation and administration. The solution should be shaken before use. Any unused product or waste material should be disposed
