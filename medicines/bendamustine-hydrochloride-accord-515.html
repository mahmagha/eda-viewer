StorageStore at temperature not above 30c and Protect from light. Precautions: -The vial is for single use Reconstitution: reconstitute each vial in 10 ml water for injection by shaking. The reconstituted concentrate appears as a clear colorless solution. Dilution: -As soon as a clear solution is obtained (usually after 5-10 minutes) dilute the total recommended dose immediately with 0.9 % Nacl solution to produce a final volume of about 500 ml. It must be diluted with 0.9 % Nacl solution and not with any other injectable solution. After reconstitution and dilution : -Chemical and physical stability has been demonstrated for 3.5 hours at 25c and 2 days at (2-8 c) in polyethylene bags. -From microbiological point of view ,the solution should be used immediately , if not used immediately ,in-use storage times and conditions prior to use are the responsibility of the user and would normally not be longer than 24 hours at (2-8 c) unless reconstitution /dilution has taken place in controlled and validated aseptic conditions .
