StorageStore at temp. not exceeding 30°C Reconstituted soln. For IV use, the reconstituted soln. should be diluted immediately after preparation. For oral use, the reconstituted soln. with purified water for oral administration is stable when stored at 2-8 °C for 48 hr. In-use stability : • for a period of 24 hr. at 25 °C, after reconstitution and dilution with sodium chloride 9 mg/ml (0.9%) or glucose soln. 50 mg/ml (5%); • for a period of 96 hr. when stored at 2-8 °C, after reconstitution and dilution with sodium chloride 9 mg/ml (0.9%) or glucose soln. 50 mg/ml (5%), or Ringer's lactate soln. or with sodium chloride 9 mg/ml (0.9%) + glucose 50 mg/ml (5%). Should be used immediately. Keep the vial in the outer carton in order to protect from light. Reconstituted soln. containing 50 mg/ml of vancomycin should be further diluted depending on the method of administration. Prepn. of the reconstituted soln. : Dissolve the powder in 20 ml of sterile Water for injection. 1 ml of reconstituted soln. contains 50 mg of vancomycin. Prepn. of final diluted Soln. for inf. Reconstituted soln. containing 50 mg/ml of vancomycin should be further diluted. Intermittent infusion : Reconstituted soln. containing 1000 mg vancomycin (50 mg/ml) must be diluted with at least 200 ml diluent (to 5 mg/ml) Continuous infusion: This should be used only if treatment with an intermittent inf. is not possible. Dilute 1000 mg to 2000 mg of dissolved vancomycin in a sufficient amount of the above suitable diluent and administer it in the form of a drip inf., so that the patient will receive the prescribed daily dose in 24 hr. Oral Adm. The contents of vials for parenteral administration may be used. The reconstituted soln. containing 1000 mg of vancomycin can be diluted in 30 ml of water and given to the patient or administered through a nasogastric tube. After Dilution :The soln. is clear and colorless without visible particles.
