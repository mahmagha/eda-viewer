StorageStore in a refrigerator (2 – 8°C), stored in the refrigerator horizontally to minimize the resuspension time. Do not freeze, discard if the vaccine has been frozen. Shake well before use. Once removed from the refrigerator, the vaccine should be used immediately. The vaccine is stable for 96 hours when stored at 8-25°C, or 72 hours when stored at 0-2°C. At the end of these time period, Prevenar 20 should be used or discard. Storage Conditions: °C
