StorageBefore Reconstitution: Store at temperature not exceeding 25°C. For intravenous administration: The contents of (1 gm) vial are reconstituted with (10 ml) water for injection, 0.9% Sodium Chloride injection or bacteriostatic water for injection then immediately diluted in (50 ml) 0.9% Sodium Chloride injection, The reconstituted IV solution should be used within 6 hours after Preparation when stored at room temperature (25 °C) or stored for 24 hours under refrigeration (5 °C) and used within 4 hours after removal from refrigeration. *Solutions of Invanz® (ertapenem sodium) should not be frozen. For intramuscular administration: The contents of (1 gm) vial are reconstituted with (3.2 ml) 1% lidocaine HCL injection (without epinephrine), The reconstituted IM solution should be used within 1 hour after Preparation.
