StorageStore at temperature not exceeding 30°C Store in original package in order to protect from light. For Intravenous Infusion: immediately before initiating IV infusion dilute one 75 mg ampoule with 100-500 ml of either 0.9% sodium chloride or 5 % glucose solution. Both solutions must be buffered with sodium bicarbonate solution (0.5 ml of 8.4% or 1 ml of 4.2%). Only clear solution should be used. Immediately infuse over 1 hour.
