# EDA drugs viewer (read-only copy)

A searchable copy of the Egyptian Drug Authority (EDA) product register: products, active ingredients,
companies, manufacturers, equivalents and summaries. It runs entirely in the browser; there is no
server behind it.

Open it at **https://mahmagha.github.io/eda-viewer/**

## Data sources

- Product records: the Egyptian Drug Authority's public drug search.
- Ingredient identifiers, structures, pharmacology and safety texts: [PubChem](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/)
  (National Center for Biotechnology Information), with each passage credited to its original source on the page.
- Identifiers and Wikipedia links: [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/) (CC0).
- Regulatory and approval data: [ChEMBL](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/) (EMBL-EBI), licensed under
  [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- Essential medicines and antibiotic groups: WHO Model List of Essential Medicines and the WHO AWaRe classification.
- Health topics: [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/) (U.S. National Library of Medicine). ICD-10-CM
  codes from the NLM [Clinical Table Search Service](https://clinicaltables.nlm.nih.gov/).
- Treatment pathways: 14 summaries written for this site from the guideline named on each one. They
  are not reviewed or endorsed by those guideline bodies.

Reference information only: not prescribing advice. Check product details with the EDA and a pharmacist.
